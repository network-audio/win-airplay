Win AirPlay 1.2.3 periodically refreshes the AirPlay input's mDNS announcement so it remains visible in the iPhone AirPlay picker without another device querying the network. Input audio capture retains the improvements verified in 1.2.2.

Download the installer for your Windows architecture below, run it, and approve the administrator prompt. Restart Windows if prompted. Logs are at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinAirPlay\controller-usbip.log` and rotate at 1 MiB, retaining at most two files.
