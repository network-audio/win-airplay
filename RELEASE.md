Win AirPlay 1.2.2 improves input audio continuity by pacing USB capture before taking PCM and buffering a small amount of incoming audio. The AirPlay service refreshes its network addresses without temporarily withdrawing its advertisement. The installer now stops the running controller and scheduled launcher before replacing a previous version.

Download the installer for your Windows architecture below, run it, and approve the administrator prompt. Restart Windows if prompted. Logs are at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinAirPlay\controller-usbip.log` and rotate at 1 MiB, retaining at most two files.
